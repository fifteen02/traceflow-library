# show_plan

A plan: its title and its steps in order, each with its status (pending, active, done, skipped, failed) and note.

## Reply

None: it only shows.

## In traceflow's apps

Plan: a shell around a panel; each step with an ink tick when done, butter when active, a pebble ring when pending; the subtitle counts them.

## On a channel without UI

The title, then a line per step: the status as a mark before it and `label — note`. Without marks (email, a terminal): `- [done] label — note`.
