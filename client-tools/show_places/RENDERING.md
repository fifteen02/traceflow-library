# show_places

Places as cards to compare and pick from, best first: each its photo, name, rating and reviews, price, kind, distance, whether it is open and its hours, with a link to its map and its phone when given. `from` puts the person on the map beside them. The person picks one by saying so, as a message.

## Reply

None: it only shows.

## In traceflow's apps

Places: a card per place with its photo and facts, Choose (which says "Let's go with <name>" as the person) and Directions, and a map with the person on it when `from` is given.

## On a channel without UI

The title, then a numbered line per place: its name; a line of facts (`★ 4.6 (1240) · €€ · Maltese · 0.4 km · Open till 22:30`); its map link. With more than one, "Reply with a number or a name to pick one."
