# show_object

A value under its label, laid out by its `schema` when given (fields with their titles), else as JSON.

## Reply

None: it only shows.

## In traceflow's apps

Object: a white panel with the label as a title and labelled fields (label faint, value regular).

## On a channel without UI

The label, then a line per field: the schema's `title` for it, else its name made readable, and its value (lists and objects joined by commas).
