# show_schedule

A day of the person's calendar as a timeline: the day, a line on what matters about it, and each event in order with its time, what it is and where. One day per call.

## Reply

None: it only shows.

## In traceflow's apps

A schedule card: the day and its summary over a timeline of the events.

## On a channel without UI

`day · summary`, then a line per event (`09:00–09:30 Standup · Meet`); "Nothing on." for a day with no events.
