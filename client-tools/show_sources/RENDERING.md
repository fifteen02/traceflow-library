# show_sources

Sources as citation cards, in order: each its title as a link to its url, the site it is on and a snippet when given.

## Reply

None: it only shows.

## In traceflow's apps

Sources: a white panel of numbered rows, the title in Medium, the site as a caption, a line between.

## On a channel without UI

The title, then a numbered line per source (`1. Title — site`), its url on the next line, and its snippet under that when it has one.
